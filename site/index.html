Methodology

Classified positions only, matched by constructor and session. Two eligible drivers per constructor; shared cars excluded. All statistics cover the 2000 season onward. Earlier results are excluded, including from career totals and Elo ratings. Missing or unclassified results are not counted. A constructor match does not prove identical equipment or strategy. Ratings use chronological Elo (1500 start, K=24), recalculated for selected filters; cross-era comparisons are approximate. Sprint and qualifying sessions have equal weight in the combined view.
